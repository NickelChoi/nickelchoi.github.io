FAQ

Is this the official test? No. Questions are original practice questions based on the official resource book Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond (© Commonwealth of Australia 2020, CC BY 4.0).

How do I pass? In the real test you need at least 75% (15 of 20) and you must answer all 5 Australian values questions correctly.
