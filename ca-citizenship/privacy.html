Privacy Policy

App: Canadian Citizenship Test (iOS)
Last updated: 20 November 2026

Summary

The app does not require an account and we do not collect your name, email or other personal details. Your quiz scores, test date and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about ad interactions and app usage to serve and measure ads. Users in the UK/EEA are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time with "Privacy & ad settings" on the home screen. On iOS, personalised ads are only shown if you allow tracking. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Notifications

If you set a test date and allow notifications, the app schedules daily study reminders locally on your device. No data is sent to us.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for the Canadian citizenship test and is not directed at children.

Content

Practice questions are original and written from facts covered in the official study guide Discover Canada. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Canada or Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).
